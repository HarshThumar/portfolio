# Ground references

What makes each of the nine real grounds recognisable. Used to build the hub miniatures (stylised handmade models, no logos, no ads, no brand names).

Facts below come from photos on Wikimedia Commons (viewed 21 Nov 2026) and, where marked "checked", a web search. Local copies are in `.tmp/ref/<slug>/` (gitignored).

## Lord's, London (`lords`)
- The Pavilion is a red-brick Victorian building with white ironwork balconies, long glazed upper floors and a dark, flat-looking roof.
- It has a small pyramid-roofed cupola on the left and a taller domed pinnacle on the right.
- The Grand Stand beside it is white, with a long cantilever roof that tilts up toward the field and a tall flagpole with stay wires.
- The Media Centre (checked) is an all-aluminium white pod, a semi-monocoque shell carried on two concrete legs, opposite the Pavilion. I did not find a clear outside photo, so its pod shape comes from the search sources.
- The Mound Stand at one end has a roof of white peaked tents.
- Stands are painted white with white seats. The outfield is striped, with a plain white boundary rail.
- Grey blocks of flats and brick houses rise behind the stands.
- Floodlights are not a feature at Lord's in these photos.

Photos: [Grand Stand](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lord's_Cricket_Ground_Grand_Stand.jpg), [Pavilion](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lords-Cricket-Ground-Pavilion-06-08-2017.jpg), [from the Media Centre](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:J_P_Morgan_Media_Centre_at_Lord%E2%80%99s_Cricket_Ground_(Ank_Kumar)_02.jpg), [Mound Stand tents](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lords_Cricket_Ground,_London_(Ank_Kumar)_01.jpg).

## Wankhede, Mumbai (`wankhede`)
- Two to three tiers of seats form a full bowl.
- A steel-truss roof ring arches over the top tier. It is blue-grey, with cream tent-shaped fabric panels under it.
- The roof edge dips and rises in a wavy line round the bowl.
- Floodlights are tall single masts with a square lamp panel on top.
- The outside is plain pale concrete with banded window strips, under the blue-grey roof trusses.
- Dark seat tiers with a green outfield. The city and a high-rise block sit behind.

Photos: [Wankhede ICC WCF](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wankhede_ICC_WCF.jpg), [dusk panorama](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wankhede_Stadium_(86312941).jpeg), [from Marine Lines](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wankhede_Stadium_as_seen_from_Marine_Lines,_Mumbai.jpg), [Wankhede Stadium](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wankhede_Stadium.jpg).

## Galle, Sri Lanka (`galle`)
- The ground is low and open. There are no big stands, only grass and a few small buildings.
- A three-storey white pavilion with blue-trimmed balconies and yellow stair accents stands at one end.
- A long, low stand with a green roof runs along another side, with a red-tile building next to it.
- The dark stone walls of the old Dutch fort rise on one side, with crenellated bastions and a small round sentry turret.
- The sea is visible beyond the town, with palm trees and trees along the boundary.
- The outfield is plain grass, with a boundary rope and little else.

Photos: [from the fort](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Galle_International_Stadium_(2).jpg), [Galle Stadium from Galle Fort](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Galle_Stadium_from_Galle_Fort.jpg), [Galle International Stadium](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Galle_International_Stadium.jpg), [Galle Fort and city](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Galle_Fort_and_city_(22759949393).jpg).

## The Oval, London (`the-oval`)
- A gasholder frame stands right behind the stands. It is a round iron cage with tall vertical columns and diagonal bracing. (Checked: the listed one is No. 1, built 1877 to 1879.)
- The seats are turquoise or aqua, in curved terraces that follow the boundary.
- The Victorian pavilion is red brick with gables, dormers and a small turret, next to a white modern stand.
- The Peter May Stand and other white stands carry cantilever roofs.
- Floodlights are multi-head masts, sometimes shaped like a tree with arms.
- Rows of brick terraced houses sit behind the stands.

Photos: [from the stands](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Oval_Cricket_Ground_-_geograph.org.uk_-_6374861.jpg), [match view](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Oval_cricket_ground_2022-08-11.jpg), [gasholder view](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Oval_Cricket_Ground_-_geograph.org.uk_-_8009756.jpg).

## Sharjah Cricket Stadium (`sharjah`)
- A compact oval bowl with low sand-coloured stands.
- A white scalloped or arched fascia runs along the roof edge of the main stand.
- Dark pyramid-shaped tents stand at one end.
- Scoreboards and screens sit on steel lattice frames.
- Floodlight towers are tall and stand outside the bowl.
- A plain beige boundary wall and a flat desert city skyline surround it.

Photos: [Sharjah Cricket Stadium](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sharjah_Cricket_Stadium.jpg), [SharjahCricket](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:SharjahCricket.JPG), [night view](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Brothers_Gas_RPL_2019_Champions.jpg).

## Eden Gardens, Kolkata (`eden-gardens`)
- A very large oval bowl with steep terraces. The seats are blue, with orange-red edges on the concrete steps.
- Grey canopy roofs cover the upper tiers on two sides. Steel trusses show under them.
- Small white peaked tents sit on one side near the pavilion.
- The floodlights are famous. They are red-and-white lattice towers with a waisted shape and a large flat panel head.
- A glass-fronted office block and a modern cantilever stand stand at the entrance.
- The outfield is wide and green, with a fence around it.

Photos: [panorama](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Eden_Garden_Stadium_Kolkata_MB01.jpg), [Eden Gardens](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Eden_Gardens.jpg), [entrance building](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Eden_Gardens_Cricket_Stadium,_Kolkata.jpg), [floodlight tower](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Iconic_Floodlight_of_The_Eden_Gardens.jpg).

## Newlands, Cape Town (`newlands`)
- A broad, steep mountain wall stands right behind the ground (Devil's Peak and the Table Mountain range). It is grey-brown rock with green lower slopes.
- Stands are low, with white tension-fabric canopies.
- Tall slender floodlight poles carry rectangular lamp heads.
- A large scoreboard screen sits at one end.
- A pavilion has a small teal cupola.
- Big trees stand behind grass areas and between stands.
- The outfield is a smooth stripe-mown lawn.

Photos: [Newlands2](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Newlands2.jpg), [Newlands Cricket Ground](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Newlands_Cricket_Ground.jpg), [view from the field](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:RSA_vs_AUS_Day_3-Newlands_Cricket_Ground_03.jpg), [floodlight at sunset](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Newlands_Cricket_New_Floodlights_Sunset.jpg).

## Melbourne Cricket Ground (`mcg`)
- A huge enclosed bowl with three or four tiers all round, grey and blue.
- A roof ring covers the top tier. Tall steel masts with stay cables stick up above the roof edge.
- Six very tall white light towers with big rectangular lamp heads stand around the bowl, arranged in pairs.
- The seats are blue-grey. The outfield is a big circle of striped grass.
- The outside is grey concrete and glass. Trees and a river are close by.

Photos: [west view](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Melbourne_Cricket_Ground,_west_remote_view_20230219_1.jpg), [inside](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Melbourne_Cricket_Ground,_Melbourne,_Victoria.jpg), [light tower at dusk](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Melbourne_Cricket_Ground_light_tower_at_dusk.jpg), [aerial](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MCG_(Melbourne_Cricket_Ground).jpg).

## HPCA Stadium, Dharamsala (`dharamsala`)
- Snow-capped mountains (the Dhauladhar range) fill the whole backdrop, with green foothills and pines.
- The pavilion has stacked red or pink pointed roofs with golden finials, like a hill temple.
- The stands are low, open terraces with seats in green, yellow, blue and pink.
- Floodlights are tall lattice masts with square heads.
- A dark scoreboard block stands at one end. A green railing runs round the boundary.
- The outfield is a bright green bowl with grassy slopes.

Photos: [HPCA Stadium Dharamsala](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HPCA_Stadium_Dharamsala.jpg), [HPCA Stadium Dharamshala](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HPCA_Stadium_Dharamshala.jpg), [snow ridge](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dharamshala_stadium,himachal_pradesh.jpg), [stormy view](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HPCA_Stadium.jpg).

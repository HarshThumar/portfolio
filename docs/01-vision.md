# 01 · Vision: "The Test Tour"

## One line
Harsh Thumar's portfolio is a cricket tour. You walk out to bat at his home ground in Ahmedabad, then travel to famous grounds around the world. Each ground holds one of his projects, and you drive a groundsman's pitch roller around it to explore.

## Who it is for
A general audience: developers, recruiters, founders, cricket fans, LinkedIn followers. Nobody should need to know what MCP or Tamarin is to enjoy it. Anyone curious can go deeper.

## What we want visitors to do (in order)
1. Follow Harsh on LinkedIn.
2. Email him.

Every ground ends with a way to do one of these. The finale (back home in Ahmedabad) does both.

## Why cricket, why Test cricket
- Harsh is a big multi-sport fan, and cricket is the one every Indian visitor reads instantly.
- **Test cricket, not T20 neon.** White kits, a red ball, wooden honours boards, pavilions and polite applause. Almost no site uses this look, so it reads as premium and timeless.
- Test cricket is the long format. It rewards patience and doing things properly. That matches how Harsh works: every claim in his credit memos has to cite its source, and his Tamarin models check a protocol 376 ways.

## The two phases (the structure Harsh asked for)
1. **The walkout (intro).** A dark stadium. Floodlights switch on one at a time. His name appears. One button: **"Walk out to bat"**. The click also unlocks audio (browsers block sound until a user gesture).
2. **The tour (main experience).** A vintage tour map, then a 3D ground per project, driven with the roller.

## The feeling we're after
- **Warm, crafted, a little funny.** Closer to a hand-made toy than a tech demo.
- **Sound everywhere.** Leather on willow, a roller rumbling over grass, a crowd that reacts to you.
- **Proof, not boasting.** Numbers are shown on old scoreboards and honours boards, and every number links to where it comes from.

## Non-goals
- Not a game with scores, levels or a fail state. It is a place to explore.
- Not an IPL/franchise site. No team colours, no official logos.
- Not a blog. No write-ups in v1.
- Not a copy of Bruno Simon's site. We take the idea "a portfolio you drive around", not his car, his art, his code or his layout.

## What success looks like
- A first-time visitor understands what to do within 5 seconds of entering.
- People screen-record it and post it. The launch LinkedIn post shows a 20-second clip.
- A recruiter in a hurry can still read everything in under 2 minutes, through the **Scorecard view** (the plain, accessible page).
- It loads fast on a mid-range Android phone on 4G, and it holds a steady frame rate there (budgets in [05-tech-architecture.md](05-tech-architecture.md)).
- Nothing on it looks or reads like AI wrote it (rules in [06-anti-slop.md](06-anti-slop.md)).
